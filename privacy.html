Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Two Step is made by Truth&Light888. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, top 10 list, missions, achievements, daily streak, gameplay stats, coins, shop items, runner names, unlocked themes and styles, and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us, and we don’t run any servers that collect them.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you’ll be asked for consent first, and you can choose non-personalized ads. If a consent choice applies to you, you can change it at any time from Privacy choices in the game’s Settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

Two Step is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.
